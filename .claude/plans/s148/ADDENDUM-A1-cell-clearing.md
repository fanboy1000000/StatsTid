## 1. Plain-language summary (read this first)

**What changes for an employee.** Today a Skema cell can be changed but never emptied. If you delete the number, the screen shows the cell empty, but nothing reaches the server, and the value comes back the next time the month loads. That has been a recorded limitation since S72 (`docs/sprints/SPRINT-72.md:56`, ruling R17). From this addendum on, emptying a cell removes the registration for real. The cell can be an absence, a project entry, or a day's work periods.

**Why now.** S148's TASK-14804 adds the rule that a day's paid absence may not exceed the day's planned hours minus the hours worked (ADR-043 D2; ADR-043 is the decision record for the overtime and flex model the owner chose in S147, and its decision D2 is this per-day cap). The rule is right, but without clearing a single typing mistake can lock a day.

**A worked example.** Mette works full time, 7,4 hours a day. On Wednesday 10 June 2026 she types 7,4 in the *Sygdom* row by mistake; she meant Thursday. She then tries to record the 7,4 hours she worked on project PRJ-1 that Wednesday.
- *After TASK-14804, without clearing:* refused. The day already holds a full paid sick day, so there is no room for work. The best she can do is lower the sickness to 0,01 and record 7,39 hours.
- *With clearing:* she deletes the 7,4 in the *Sygdom* cell and leaves the cell. A second later the removal is saved. She records 7,4 hours on PRJ-1, and it is accepted. The record keeps both facts: at 09:12 Mette registered 7,4 hours of sickness on 10 June, and at 09:14 Mette removed it.
- Had the mistaken cell been a *Ferie* day, the removal would also give the holiday day back to her balance, and the balance history would show −1,0 day.
- Had it been a 0,01-hour project entry on a day she wants as a care day (*omsorgsdag*), she clears the entry and then books the care day. Since owner Q1 (2026-11-10), a care day goes only on a day with no work.
- HR can do the same for her, as HR can already change her cells.
- Work periods: a day is removed only when every period row is gone. A day whose periods are all half-typed (a start with no end yet) is not removed; it keeps its stored value until the periods are complete or removed.
- A child's first sick day cannot be removed while the second day stands, because the second day is only valid after the first. To remove a two-day illness, remove day 2 first.

**What does not change.**
- A month that has been sent or approved stays locked. A removal is refused exactly as a change is (409).
- Only those who may change a cell may empty it: the employee, or HR and admins in scope. A manager still cannot edit an employee's registrations (S124), and someone who has left cannot change their own timesheet (S147).
- A cell still never stores 0 hours. On the screen, 0 or empty means "remove". On the server, a removal is its own instruction.
- A care day or senior day is still a whole day. It is removed whole and never lowered.
- Payslips: nothing changes for the demo (PIN-1, the sprint's pay pin: a test that runs payroll on every approved demo month and must produce byte-for-byte the same payslips after every change). Payroll already reads only current registrations; a removed one stops being current. A calculation made before the removal can still be replayed from the rows it used.
- Nothing disappears from the audit trail. The removed registration is kept, marked as retired by the removal, and the removal names who made it and when. This holds for all three cell types, a day's work periods included.
- A removal stays removed across a Backend restart, even one that lands before the removal has been copied to the permanent event log. One limit is registered rather than fixed here (O-31): if the read tables were ever emptied and rebuilt from scratch, any registration or removal not yet copied to the event log would be missing until the next restart after it is copied. That gap already exists today for every registration.

**The balance record (owner Q1, 2026-11-11).** Each employee has one balance record per holiday type and year, holding how many days are used. In a rare state that record is missing although a holiday is booked: the holiday was booked while the type had no configuration, and the configuration was added later. Today the system then creates the record at "0 used" on the next change, which under-counts; this has been a registered defect since S145 (QUAL-196). Clearing would make it visible: Mette, in that state with one *Ferie* day booked, removes it and ends at −1 day used, so the screen offers her one day more than she is entitled to. The owner chose to fix it in S148: a missing record is created from her actual current bookings, in every place that creates one, so the same removal ends at 0 used. **The record's total, too (owner Q4, 2026-11-11, "Include it").** A record also holds the year's entitlement, its "total" (25 holiday days for a full year). One of the four places that create a record, the recalculation that runs when HR backdates a change to someone's working hours, creates it with a total of 0, so the team overview would show her holiday as "of 0 days" and her remaining days as negative for good, because nothing later corrects a total. A second, the year-end settlement, creates next year's record when it carries holiday days over, and fills in the total from the year being closed: if the entitlement changed when the new year began (say from 25 to 26 days), the new year would keep 25 for good. From TASK-14842 on, every place that creates a record gives it the year's real entitlement. All four read it the same way, as the entitlement in force on the first day of that record's own holiday year, so whichever of them happens to create the record, the total is the same. (Only an entitlement scaled to part-time hours could still differ, by which part-time fraction each place applies; no entitlement is configured that way today.)

**What it costs.** One backend task with its test pass and one stacked frontend task, in wave 2 after the children's-sick-days stack (14811/14812), and, before the clearing tasks, one backend task with its test pass that fixes the balance record (TASK-14842). Clearing adds three small event types, one audit row per removal, one replay rule for the projection rebuild that runs at every Backend start, and one new column on the work-time table: a cleared work-time day is kept and marked, as the other two cell types already are, instead of being deleted. *Why the column:* a deleted day would lose the one thing that stops an older registration being replayed over it when the Backend restarts (D-4).

## 2. Design

*Each decision gives the lean (or, from draft 1.1, the ruling), the alternatives and what is given up. "Posted" means a value the request saves; "cleared" means a cell the request empties.*

### D-1 — How a clear is expressed in the save request

**Lean: one explicit `cleared` member on the existing save request, holding three typed arrays that mirror the three posted arrays.**
```json
{ "year": 2026, "month": 6,
  "entries": [...], "absences": [...], "workTime": [...],
  "cleared": {
    "entries":  [ { "date": "2026-06-10", "projectCode": "PRJ-1" } ],
    "absences": [ { "date": "2026-06-10", "absenceType": "SICK_DAY" } ],
    "workTime": [ { "date": "2026-06-11" } ] } }
```
- *Where:* `SaveSkemaRequest` gains `Cleared` beside `Entries`, `Absences` and `WorkTime` (`SkemaEndpoints.cs:2956-2969`), with three item types beside `SkemaEntry`, `SkemaWorkTimeDay` and `SkemaAbsence` (`:2971-3000`). The cell keys are the ones the save already replaces by: (date, project code), (date, absence type), and the day for work time (`AbsenceProjectionRepository.cs:37-45`; `TimeEntryProjectionRepository.cs:47-55`; the work-time row's key, `docker/postgres/init.sql:1973-1984`).
- *Why typed arrays:* each item's required members are enforced by the type, so there is no "exactly one of project code or absence type" rule to validate. They mirror the arrays the frontend already builds.
- *A 0-hour row stays impossible.* The R17 400 for posted hours ≤ 0 stays (`SkemaEndpoints.cs:818-826`, `:830-838`), pinned by `SkemaCellSupersessionTests.Resave_ZeroOrNegativeHours_Returns400_WritesNothing` (`tests/StatsTid.Tests.Regression/Skema/SkemaCellSupersessionTests.cs:313`). A clear inserts no row at all (D-4).
- *Alternative A: a flat `cleared` list with a `kind` discriminator.* Same meaning, one array. Rejected for the validation it adds and a discriminated union in the generated client.
- *Alternative B: `hours: 0` (or null) reinterpreted as "remove".* Rejected. It gives 0 two meanings, a typo and an intent. It turns a deliberate, pinned refusal into a hidden instruction, so a hand-built or stale request carrying 0 would delete silently. And every guard that reads posted hours would need a "0 means remove" branch: the all-types cap (`:1029-1090`), the full-day guard (`:1113-1157`), the bound (`:1805-1899`) and the quota pre-check (`:1347-1761`).
- *Response:* unchanged. `saved` keeps counting registration events (`:2572`); a clear-only request answers `saved: 0`, and its effect is read back from the month GET. Adding a `cleared` count would change a second contract for no screen that needs it.
- *Unknown member on today's code:* the host sets no `UnmappedMemberHandling` (`git grep` over `src` finds none), so a `cleared` member is ignored today. That is why every Docker fact below is red on base (kind F): the request answers 200 and nothing is cleared.

**Validation added before anything else happens** (beside the existing shape checks, before the rule-engine call, the lock and the transaction, so nothing is written on refusal). Bodies carry the request's own date, never an employment date (ADR-040 D7).
1. **Month bound:** every cleared date lies in the requested month, else 400 `skema_cell_date_out_of_range`, the siblings' body (`:854-871`). This is the reason the save month-bounds every array: the supersession mark carries no period condition (`:841-853`).
2. A cleared entry with a blank project code gives 400 `skema_entry_project_code_missing` (`:811-817`).
3. A cleared absence code outside the vocabulary gives 422 `absence_type_unknown`, the same gate as a posted code (`:968-977`). It is the shared vocabulary only: a clear is never refused because the agreement no longer offers the code (14811's per-agreement offer check judges posted rows).
4. A cell both posted and cleared, or cleared twice, in one request gives 400 (`skema_cell_cleared_and_posted`, `skema_cell_cleared_twice`), naming the date and the cell key.
5. **A posted work-time day with no positive-duration interval gives 400 `skema_work_time_day_empty`** ("remove the day with `cleared.workTime`"). This is W-a (§ 8, NR-A3 confirmed). Once 14809 retires manual hours, it is the work-time form of R17, and it closes today's 0-hour work-time row (see BC-A3).

The eligibility and age gates (`:1159-1259`), the weekend refusal (`:1064-1077`), the full-day guard and the profile-missing 422 judge posted rows only. A clear removes what is stored; none of them applies to it.

### D-2 — The locks, the month bound, the employment window: a clear goes through the save's gates unchanged

The clear is a member of the save request, so it passes every gate the save has, in the save's order, before any write. The only code change is to add the cleared dates where a gate collects dates.

| Gate | Where | What the clear needs |
|---|---|---|
| Employee own data; HR floor for others (S124); terminated-inclusive scope validator | `SkemaEndpoints.cs:662-706` | nothing (runs before the body is read) |
| Departed-subject floor, advisory and in-lock (S147) | `:749-752`, `:2018-2027` | nothing |
| Month approval lock, advisory | `:787-789` (`ApprovalPeriodSaveLock.IsPeriodLockedForSave`: `EMPLOYEE_APPROVED` or `APPROVED`, `src/Backend/StatsTid.Backend.Api/ApprovalPeriodSaveLock.cs:29-30`) | nothing: it refuses any request to a locked month, clears included |
| Month bound | `:854-889` | rule 1 above |
| Per-employee lock, READ COMMITTED | `:1980`, `:1988-1989` | the in-lock decisions in D-4 run under it |
| Employment-window gate, in-lock | `:2068-2086` | **the cleared dates join `registrationDates` (`:2068-2074`)**, so a clear dated outside the window answers the shared date-free 422 |
| Approval re-read, in-lock | `:2110-2116` | nothing: it precedes every write, so a send that commits while the clear waits wins with 409 |

### D-3 — The events

**Ruled (NR-A4 confirmed): three new event types, one per cell family, mirroring the three registration events and the three projections:** `TimeEntryCleared`, `AbsenceCleared` and `WorkTimeCleared`, in `src/SharedKernel/StatsTid.SharedKernel/Events/`.

| Event | Payload (beside `DomainEventBase`'s `EventId`, `OccurredAt`, `ActorId`, `ActorRole`, `CorrelationId`) |
|---|---|
| `TimeEntryCleared` | `EmployeeId`, `Date`, `TaskId` (the project code), `ClearedEventId` (the `TimeEntryRegistered` whose row this clear retires), `ClearedHours` |
| `AbsenceCleared` | `EmployeeId`, `Date`, `AbsenceType`, `ClearedEventId` (the `AbsenceRegistered`), `ClearedHours`, `ClearedFeriedage` (nullable: the stored feriedage given back; null for a type that consumes nothing) |
| `WorkTimeCleared` | `EmployeeId`, `Date`, `ClearedEventId` (the latest `WorkTimeRegistered` for the day: the event of the row's `outbox_id`, **read in the lock before the clear advances that `outbox_id` to its own** (D-4; adjudication edit 3); `work_time_projection` keeps `outbox_id`, `init.sql:1982`, and `outbox_events` keeps `event_id`, `init.sql:59-75`), `ClearedHours` (interval hours) |

- *Why typed events:* each family's identity is a required member, so no event can carry the wrong key. They read plainly in the audit trail ("AbsenceCleared").
- *Stream and ordering:* all three ride `employee-{employeeId}` (`SkemaEndpoints.cs:1923`), the stream of the registrations they retire. Each is enqueued first, which allocates its outbox id, and the projection change comes second, inside the save's one transaction (the save's rule, `:1934-1938`; ADR-018 D3). Per-stream FIFO (ADR-018 D5) puts a clear after the registration it retires, because that registration committed earlier under the same per-employee lock and so holds a smaller outbox id. A balance event from the same save follows on the same stream. Either the whole save commits or none of it does.
- *Registration:* `EventSerializer.cs` gains the three names beside `:18`, `:26` and `:148` (DEP-003). `EventSerializerCoverageTests.EventSerializer_RegistersAllDomainEventBaseDescendants` (`tests/StatsTid.Tests.Unit/Events/EventSerializerCoverageTests.cs:16`) turns red if one is missing.
- *Alternative E1: one `SkemaCellCleared` with a `CellKind` discriminator.* It needs one mapper and one catalog row instead of three, but its key members become nullable with an "exactly one" rule. A close second, declined (§ 8, NR-A4).
- *Alternative E3: a "removed" variant of the registration events* (`AbsenceRegistered` with `Hours = 0` or a `Retracted` flag). Rejected. It is the 0-hour registration R17 forbids, now in the event store, and every reader of registration events would have to learn that a registration can mean a removal: the rebuild (`ProjectionBackfillService.cs:412-516`) and payroll's frozen-row digests (`src/SharedKernel/StatsTid.SharedKernel/Segmentation/PlanInputs.cs`).

### D-4 — The projection effect, the in-lock decision, and the rebuild

**Project entries and absences: mark, insert nothing.** Inside the lock, in the order fixed below ("In-lock order"), for each cleared cell:
1. The cell's current Skema row comes from the one authoritative in-lock read (step 1 of the in-lock order), on the save's own (conn, tx). For an absence: `GetCurrentSkemaRowsAsync` (`AbsenceProjectionRepository.cs:257-266`). For an entry: the (conn, tx) current-rows read (`TimeEntryProjectionRepository.cs:160`), filtered to source `SKEMA` and the task.
2. **No current row: a no-op.** No event, no audit row, no balance change. A removal of nothing records nothing.
3. **One row:** enqueue the `…Cleared` event (its outbox id), then retire the row with **the existing mark SQL** and the clear's outbox id (`TimeEntryProjectionRepository.cs:47-55`, `AbsenceProjectionRepository.cs:37-45`) through a new repository method that returns the rows marked. Exactly one must be marked; anything else throws, and the save rolls back. The row is kept with `superseded_by_outbox_id` = the clear's outbox id. No row is inserted. The column has no foreign key (`init.sql:1902`, `:1946`), so pointing it at an event with no projection row is legal.
4. The decision, and every value the clear carries (`ClearedEventId`, `ClearedHours`, `ClearedFeriedage`), is taken **in the lock**. Two concurrent clears of one cell therefore serialise: the second finds no current row and does nothing, so a day is given back once (AC-CL-23). A clear queued behind a re-save of the same cell retires the re-saved row and gives back its feriedage, and a re-save queued behind a clear leaves its own row current (AC-CL-34, -35). The pre-transaction reads are advisory only (D-6, D-8).

Only Skema rows are retired, as the mark SQL already says (`source = 'SKEMA'`). TASK-14802 retired `POST /api/time-entries` (merged `e4e6b0ea`); after the reseed no `TIME_POST` row exists (adjudication, `S148-TASK-14804-14806-adjudication.md:83`).

**Every reader already ignores a retired row.** Each read that totals or shows hours filters `superseded_by_outbox_id IS NULL`:
- both repositories' default reads (`AbsenceProjectionRepository.cs:173-179`, `:202-208`; `TimeEntryProjectionRepository.cs:180-182`, `:210-212`);
- the team overview and the allocation reads (`ApprovalEndpoints.cs:1159-1161`, `:1180-1183`, `:1265-1267`, `:1607-1610`, `:1653-1656`);
- the strand check (`EmploymentWindowStrandCheck.cs:65-70`);
- the vacation settlement (`VacationSettlementService.cs:1921-1928`);
- payroll's current read (`ProjectionHoursReader.cs:98-101`).

The one unfiltered read is by event id, for replay (`AbsenceProjectionRepository.cs:221-243`). This is the room S145 left for this design (ADR-028 D6, `docs/knowledge-base/decisions/ADR-028-work-time-persistence-allocation-gate-timer-retirement.md:108`).

**A work-time day: the row is kept and marked** (adjudication item 1, B-1 fixed by option (c)). `work_time_projection` is latest-wins: a re-save overwrites the row in place (`WorkTimeProjectionRepository.cs:76-113`; ADR-028 D1). Draft 1 deleted the row on a clear. That loses the one thing that stops an older registration being replayed over the day when the Backend restarts (the rebuild, below), so the row is kept, as the other two cell types keep theirs:
- **One new column,** `cleared_by_outbox_id BIGINT NULL`, on `work_time_projection` (`docker/postgres/init.sql:1973-1984`; reseed, don't migrate; the `init.sql` approval, § 8). Null means the day is current.
- **The clear is a guarded update:** `SET cleared_by_outbox_id = @clear, outbox_id = @clear WHERE employee_id = @e AND date = @d AND outbox_id <= @clear`. Live, exactly one row with a null marker must be updated; anything else throws and the save rolls back. *Why the clear advances `outbox_id` to its own:* the upsert's guard is `<=` (`WorkTimeProjectionRepository.cs:101`; `ProjectionBackfillService.cs:234`), so a replayed registration carrying the row's old id would pass it and un-clear the day (M-20).

A day registered again after its clear is current again; the `outbox_id <=` guard keeps an older replay from doing the same.
- **"Current" for work time means a row with a null marker. Every reader filters `cleared_by_outbox_id IS NULL`** (six sites, plus the bound's read):
  - the team overview's worked hours (`ApprovalEndpoints.cs:1201`);

**In-lock order** (adjudication item 3, N-2). Two facts force it. The balance block sits inside `if (request.Absences is not null)` (`SkemaEndpoints.cs:2378`) and loops over posted types only (`:2443`). And the replaced read (`:2384`) reads current rows, so a clear that retired its row first would vanish from the balance delta. The order:
1. After the approval re-read (`:2110-2116`), **one authoritative read** on (conn, tx) of every cell the request posts or clears, before the first retire or insert. **No read of "current" after the first retire** (cycle-2 ruling R3; Codex and Fable c2 N-2). TASK-14842's seed aggregation in step 5 (the Σ of current rows, when a balance record is missing) is a separate read, not this snapshot, and is allowed.
2. The in-lock guards run on the resulting state from that read: the all-types cap, the full-day guard, the bound (D-8) and the chain (D-9). The bound keeps its own reads (`SkemaEndpoints.cs:1819-1835`), all taken before the first write (D-8).
3. **The clears:** for each cleared cell with a current row, enqueue the `…Cleared` event, then retire with the one-row check. Each clear's `ClearedEventId`, `ClearedHours` and `ClearedFeriedage` come from step 1's read.
4. **The posted writes,** in today's order (entries `:2315`, work time `:2352`, absences `:2378`).
5. **One balance pass** over the union of posted and cleared entitlement types (D-6). It runs even when `request.Absences` is null.

**The rebuild must replay the clears. This is a never-extra.** `ProjectionBackfillService` runs **at every Backend start** over the populated tables (`ProjectionBackfillService.cs:23-30`; Backend `Program.cs:565-573`, before `app.Run()` at `:611`) and replays registrations idempotently (`:148-160`, `:370-531`). It reads only `events` (`:148-160`), while a save writes only `outbox_events` (`PostgresEventStore.cs:278-289`); `events` is filled later by the publisher, which starts with the host. So a clear may not be in `events` yet when the rebuild runs. Two safety properties hold, and one limit is registered:
- **Populated tables are safe because every cleared row is kept.** An entry or absence replay conflicts on `event_id` (`ProjectionBackfillService.cs:182`, `:195`). A work-time replay meets the kept row, whose `outbox_id` the clear advanced, so the `<=` guard (`:234`) refuses the older registration. This holds while the clear is still unpublished, and while a rebuild runs beside live traffic, which the service is designed for (`:41-45`, "safer for online use"; the ops tool is its second caller): a clear that commits after the rebuild's read but before its upsert still meets the kept row.
- **From empty tables, the three replay branches rebuild the clears:**
  - the three types join the replay's event-type list (`:158`);
  - `TimeEntryCleared` and `AbsenceCleared` run the same mark SQL with the clear's outbox id (`:284-297`);
  - `WorkTimeCleared` replays the guarded update, its `outbox_id <= @clear` included. Live, that guard is always true under the lock; only the replay needs it: a replayed clear must not retire a day registered again after it whose re-registration is not yet published (cycle-2 ruling R6, Fable c2 W-2; AC-CL-38, M-21);
  - a counter for each (the result record, `:86-105`; optional members as in S145).
- **The registered limit (O-31):** from empty tables, a committed fact of any replayed type that is not yet published is absent until the next start after it is published, or indefinitely on a quarantined stream (`OutboxPublisher.cs:195-202`). The gap exists today for registrations; it is registered, not built here.

The existing guards keep it right on a re-run: the mark's `superseded_by_outbox_id IS NULL AND outbox_id < @outboxId` (`AbsenceProjectionRepository.cs:44-45`), and the work-time update's `outbox_id <=` guard. A cell registered again after its clear stays current, because its row's outbox id is larger. A re-run over populated tables changes nothing.

### D-5 — The audit mapping and the catalog row

**Ruled (NR-A5 confirmed): the three clear events are audit-relevant.** The audit read is HR-and-above in scope (`AuditEndpoints.cs:32`, `:66`), and the audit row is written in the save's transaction, so the restart question (B-1) does not touch it. ### D-6 — Holiday-type cells: the days are given back, with the balance event (ADR-032)

**Lean: a clear of a quota-consuming absence moves `used` down by the row's stored feriedage. It goes through the existing ungated reduction in the save's transaction and emits one `EntitlementBalanceAdjusted` per entitlement type.** It reuses S145's signed-delta machinery as it stands:

- delta ≤ 0 uses `AdjustUsedAsync`, ungated (`:2473-2483`; `EntitlementBalanceRepository.cs:130-160`);

What the clear adds:
- **The retired rows count as replaced.** `ReadReplacedFeriedageByTypeAsync` keys on the posted cells (`SkemaEndpoints.cs:1330-1331`) and now also on the cleared absence cells, in both its reads: advisory (`:1661`) and in-lock (`:2384`), the latter being step 1 of D-4's in-lock order, taken before any retire.
- **A cleared type gets a plan even when the request posts no absence of that type.** The pre-check runs only when the request posts absences (`:1347`), and only for posted types (`:1364-1389`, `:1420`). The in-lock balance pass visits the type: it is D-4's step 5, one pass over the union of posted and cleared types, which runs even when `request.Absences` is null (today's loop sits inside that `if`, `:2378`, `:2443`).

### D-8 — The paid-absence bound (TASK-14804)

**Ruled (NR-A6 confirmed (a)): a date that carries a posted cell is judged on the day as it will stand after the save, with cleared cells removed.
