## Plain language — why

**What goes wrong today.** Since TASK-14820 (wave 4), moving a person to another organisation is a dated change, and the server refuses a move dated in the future: until that day, the person's access would follow the new organisation while their dated record still showed the old one (C-13, `REF:544`). But the edit-person drawer has one date field for the whole save, built for scheduling future changes of agreement code and part-time (S141), and it still offers any date. So HR's combined save "move to institution B and go part-time from 1 December" fails as a whole, and the screen shows the server's English text, "Transfer date validation failed" (`useEditPerson.ts:239-249` passes only the error's message to the drawer). The picker's own text also says the organisation "is saved at once, regardless of this date", which has been false since wave 4 (`EffectiveDatePicker.tsx:200-218`, `:243-245`).

**What this task does** (NR-P4, the rule wave 6 ends with: a screen never offers an action the server refuses).
1. **While the organisation field is changed, the drawer offers no date after today and says why in Danish** (AC-C13j); with the organisation unchanged, future dates stay offered (S141's scheduling).
2. **The picker's label, helper and future notice say what is true** (ruling 36): the date also dates an organisation move, which cannot be in the future; name, e-mail and management are still saved at once.
3. **Both transfer refusals are shown in Danish, with their date** (ruling 37; AC-C13u): a future date that slips through (a race, an old tab), and a date before the start of the person's current organisation (possible only after an earlier dated move), which names the earliest date the move can take. *Given up:* two hook files join the task.
4. **One's own record stays locked** (X7): on the logged-in HR user's own record the date field stays disabled, linked to the self-lock note, with its helper hidden (TASK-14846).

**Worked example (AC-C13j).** Today is Tuesday 10 November 2026. Lise (LocalHR) opens Jonas, changes his organisation and picks Tuesday 1 December 2026: the save is blocked, the date input's `max` is `2026-11-10`, and item 6's "Drawer, NR-P4 block" sentence shows. With the organisation unchanged, Tuesday 1 December 2026 stays offered and the save sends `effectiveFrom: "2026-12-01"`. **(AC-C13u)** If the server still answers `BEFORE_CURRENT_ORGANISATION` with `nearestValid` `["2026-06-15"]` (Jonas joined his current organisation on Monday 15 June 2026), the drawer shows item 6's `BEFORE_CURRENT_ORGANISATION` sentence with `{dato}` as "15. juni 2026", never the English text.
