## Plain language — why

After TASK-14826 the Rule Engine refuses any request that does not carry the agreement rules; TASK-14827 made payroll send them. Four Backend and Orchestrator callers remain, and two screen figures that never asked the engine at all.

1. **The compliance check** (the 11-hour rest warnings on Skema and Teamoversigt) must send the rules in force on each day it checks, so an administrator's edit to the minimum rest reaches it. Example: HK publishes a 12-hour minimum from Monday 16 March 2026; the March check now judges a Monday work end against 12 hours and a Sunday work end against 11.
2. **The overtime-governance warning and the Orchestrator's weekly pipeline** are legacy paths that retire in stage 2 (S1-4). The owner's ruling (R-8) is that they keep sending **the compiled rules**, each under a `shortcut(pending)` comment that the Orchestrator registers, rather than learning the dated lookup only to be deleted.
3. **The two month figures** ("expected hours this month" on Teamoversigt and on the balance page) are worked out today as weekdays × one day's norm, with the weekly norm taken at the end of the month. Payroll adds up each day's own norm. Once a dated configuration can change on a Monday mid-month the two disagree, even for a full-timer: a weekly norm going from 37 to 32 hours on Monday 16 March 2026 would show **140.80 h** (22 × 6.40) while payroll judges **150.80 h** (10 × 7.40 + 12 × 6.40). From this task both screens add up each day's configured norm, at the part-time fraction of the month's last day (the owner's QUAL-198 ruling, kept: NR-20 (b)), so they show **150.80 h**.
4. **The Skema month** now serves the minimum rest in force on each day, so the screen's instant warning (TASK-14829) can use it instead of a fixed 11 hours.

**What does not change.** No payslip (this task touches no payroll file); the compliance check's window, leader rule and arithmetic; Teamoversigt's and the balance's other figures; the organisation-drift guards TASK-14845 put in three of your files. On the seeded data every configuration equals the compiled one, every seeded agreement is 37 hours in both OK versions (`docker/postgres/init.sql:1625-1682` at `bb529e62`; BC-P10 cites `:1452-1508` at `fee2a635`), so every existing figure stays as it is (the two 132.00 h pins, AC-C1n (3)).
