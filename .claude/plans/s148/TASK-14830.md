## Plain language — why

**The problem.** Every payroll calculation stores a "proof" beside its result (the manifest's frozen inputs), so that anyone can later re-run the month (a **replay**) and check that the figures are what the rules give. Until now the agreement rules were treated as *code*: the proof stored only a fingerprint (a hash) of them, and a replay looked the rules up again and refused if the hash had changed. Since the config stage (ADR-043 D12) the rules are *data* that administrators edit and date: a new version can apply from a day in the past. A hash can say "something changed", but it cannot give back the rules a month was paid on, and since TASK-14827 a replay has to look them up again from frozen keys, which is exactly the step that can drift.

**What this task does.** Each calculation now stores, in its proof, **the full set of agreement rules it actually sent to the engine, one copy per stretch of days** (a run: the same agreement, OK version, position and institution, between the same two days). A replay hands the engine exactly those stored rules and never asks the configuration database again. The fingerprint, its check and the refusal `replay-config-changed` disappear, because there is nothing left to fingerprint. A proof written before this change lacks the stored rules, so a replay of it is refused by name ("the manifest lacks a frozen input the current contract requires") instead of being re-run on today's rules.

**Worked example.** Mette is on HK in institution STY01. Her June 2026 (OK26) is calculated on the published HK OK26 version: 37 hours a week.
- On Tuesday 10 November 2026 a global administrator publishes a new HK OK26 version **dated Monday 1 June 2026**: 32 hours.
- **Before this task**, a replay of June looks the rules up again as of 1 June, finds 32 hours, sees the hash differ and refuses (`replay-config-changed`): the proof of June can no longer be re-run, although June was paid correctly on what was in force when it was calculated.
- **After this task**, June's proof holds the 37-hour version by value. The replay sends exactly that and reproduces June to the byte. The 32-hour version reaches June only through an explicit correction (the worklist row TASK-14823's hand-off writes, then `/recalculate`), never by the publish and never by a replay (ADR-043 D12: "a publish never changes an exported month").

**What does not change.** The arithmetic, the plan and its runs (TASK-14827's), what payroll sends on a forward calculation, every demo payslip (PIN-1), the evaluation marker (`weekly-timeline-v3`: the replay contract's change is refused by the frozen-inputs reason, not by the marker; P-4, R-24, `REF:471`), and the wage-type mapping, which a replay still reads dated as the forward run did (ADR-016 D10's recorded exception).
