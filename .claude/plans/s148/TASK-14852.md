## Plain language — why

**What HR sees today.** When a person's employment profile has been closed, the drawer's "Profil" section shows the fraction and position fields greyed out with a hover text, "Profil ikke fundet for denne medarbejder" (`frontend/src/pages/admin/editPerson/ProfileSection.tsx:37-38`), and nothing else. HR cannot type into them, the drawer's save skips the profile (`frontend/src/hooks/useEditPerson.ts:289`), and nothing explains what happened or what to do. Meanwhile payroll refuses the person's months and HR's "cannot register" list says "Skal rettes".

**What this task builds** (the owner's "the drawer offers 'create profile'").
1. **The drawer says what is true, in Danish.** No profile from a named date; or a profile planned from a named date (nothing to do); or not possible yet, and why.
2. **"Opret ny profil" appears only when the server says a re-create is possible** (the GET's `canCreate`, TASK-14851's BC-6), never on a failed read, and never enabled on one's own record (X7: the server refuses it).
3. **A small form with its own save.** The date is pre-set to the first day without a profile, so the usual repair leaves no gap and no refused month; it can be moved later but never after today, and a later date says plainly which days will have no profile. The fraction and position are pre-filled from the last profile, so undoing a mistaken close is one click.
4. **After the save, the drawer is an ordinary edit again**, holding the new version token, so the next "Gem ændringer" does not fail as stale.
5. **Every refusal reads as a Danish sentence**, never the server's English.
6. **HR's "cannot register" list names the new action** in its note.

**Worked example** (today Friday 10 April 2026; Lise, LocalHR; Anna's profile closed on Monday 16 March 2026).
- Lise opens Anna. The Profil section reads (proposed copy) "Medarbejderen har ingen ansættelsesprofil fra og med 16. marts 2026. Uden en profil kan lønnen ikke beregnes for de dage." and shows **Opret ny profil**.
- She clicks it. The form shows the date **16. marts 2026** (earliest 16 March, latest 10 April), Deltidsfraktion **1,000** and an empty Stilling (Anna's last profile). She changes the fraction to 0,800, the position to "Fuldmægtig", and the date to **25. marts 2026**; a notice appears: "Dagene fra og med 16. marts 2026 til og med 24. marts 2026 får ingen profil …".
- She saves. The drawer sends one `PUT …/employee-profiles/<Anna>` with `If-None-Match: *` and `{ effectiveFrom: "2026-03-25", partTimeFraction: 0.8, position: "Fuldmægtig" }`; on 200 the section shows 0,800 and "Fuldmægtig" as editable fields again, and a toast says "Profilen er oprettet."
- Had the server answered 400 `BEFORE_LAST_PROFILE_END` with `["2026-03-16"]`, the form would say "Den nye profil kan tidligst gælde fra 16. marts 2026." Opening her own closed profile, Lise sees the button greyed out, pointing at the drawer's self-lock note.
