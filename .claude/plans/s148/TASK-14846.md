## Plain language — why

**The problem.** Since S148 the server refuses an HR user or administrator who tries to change their own pay inputs and entitlements: their organisation, agreement code, part-time fraction, position, employment start date, birth date and child's-sick-day eligibility. The rule is right: nobody signs off their own pay (the S147 four-eyes ruling; RES-003). But the edit drawer does not know it. When Lise, an HR user at STY01, opens **her own** record and changes, say, her agreement code, the save fails and the drawer shows the server's raw English answer, a JSON object, on a Danish screen (`frontend/src/lib/api.ts:615-632`: the error message is the response body's text).

**What this task does** (the owner's answer of 2026-11-18, option (a)). When the drawer's person is the logged-in user, the drawer **locks every input the server would refuse** and shows **one Danish note** explaining why and who can do it instead. It locks nothing else, so Lise can still correct her own name or e-mail and save, and her save sends exactly what it sends today. The termination page already does this for one's own end date (`TerminationPage.tsx:75`, `:263-268`).

**Worked example.** Lise (LocalHR, STY01) opens her own record.
- **Today:** the organisation, agreement, fraction, position, start-date, birth-date and child's-sick fields are all editable. She changes her agreement from HK to AC and clicks "Gem ændringer": the users PUT answers 403 and the drawer shows `{"error":"Access denied","reason":"Own agreement code and employee profile cannot be modified; …"}`.
- **After this task:** those seven inputs and the effective-date picker are disabled (visible, greyed), and so are the two "carry this change forward to the scheduled change" checkboxes (the server carries a change forward only after a real change of the field, which it refuses her; left enabled they would do nothing and mislead). A note, styled as information rather than as an error, says: "Du kan ikke selv ændre de låste felter, fordi de har betydning for din egen løn eller dine rettigheder. Bed en anden HR-administrator om at gøre dette." She fixes a typo in her e-mail and saves: the users PUT carries the new e-mail and her unchanged organisation and agreement code, which the server lets through.
- **Another HR user** opening Lise's record sees no note and nothing locked by this rule.

**What does not change.** The server (no route, no body); the generated client; anyone else's record; what a save sends.
