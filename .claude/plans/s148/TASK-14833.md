## Plain language — why

**What a LocalAdmin gets.** The local-profile editor (mounted on the configuration page, `ConfigManagement.tsx`) shows today four fields an institution may override. TASK-14832 lets the server store eighteen more: the institution's own flex rules, its office hours per weekday, and the basis on which a day of paid absence is credited. This task puts them on the screen, saves them, and shows the server's refusals in Danish under the right field.

**Three things the screen must say.**
1. **"Saved, but not yet used."** None of the new settings affects any calculation or pay until stage 1 (S149), or stage 2 for office hours' filter (`REF:358`). Each new field carries a short note saying so, as the two overtime fields already carry theirs (TASK-14829). An administrator must not believe a change to the plus cap moved anyone's pay this month.
2. **AC's "default by analogy".** For staff on the AC agreement no central text says how a paid sick day counts. StatsTid applies the HK and PROSA rule (§ 7 nr. 2) as a default "by analogy", labelled so (ADR-043 D2, owner ruling D-8b = (a)). On an AC profile, the editor shows that label beside the default credit basis.
3. **A warning, never a refusal, about office hours.** If the office hours entered do not add up to the weekly norm (37 hours, or the profile's own weekly norm where one is set), the editor says so, and still saves. Office hours that differ from the norm can be legitimate (ADR-043 D3; provisional item 14 asks an expert how they scale for part-timers).

**Worked example.** STY01's LocalAdmin opens the AC/OK24 profile. Below the existing fields a new card lists the seven flex settings, office hours per weekday and the credit basis, each with the same small note. She enters Monday to Thursday 08:00–15:30 and Friday 08:00–14:00. That is 36 hours; the profile sets no weekly norm of its own, so the editor compares with 37 and shows the warning (BC-1's text, for example "Kontortiden er i alt 36 timer om ugen, men normen er 37 timer. Du kan stadig gemme."). She picks "Kontortid" as the credit basis; because this is an AC profile, the analogy label sits beside "§ 7 nr. 2". She saves: the request goes through (the server never refuses a sum). Had she picked "Kontortid" with no office hours at all, the server would refuse (400, `OFFICE_HOURS_WITHOUT_WINDOWS`) and the editor would show the Danish message under the credit basis, linked to it for a screen reader.

**What does not change.** The four existing fields, their "Overskriv central" toggles, the two overtime notes (NR-P13), the Monday-only date picker (it still follows the weekly norm only), the 412 banner and the history view.
