## Plain language — why

**What a LocalAdmin gets.** The local-profile editor (mounted on the configuration page, `ConfigManagement.tsx`) shows today four fields an institution may override. TASK-14832 lets the server store eighteen more: the institution's own flex rules, its office hours per weekday, and the basis on which a day of paid absence is credited. This task puts them on the screen, saves them, and shows the server's refusals in Danish under the right field. It also lists them in the profile history on the same page, which otherwise would show a profile holding only new settings as four times "(arvet fra central)" (ruling 19).

**Four things the screen must say.**
1. **"Saved, but not yet used."** None of the new settings affects any calculation or pay until stage 1 (S149), or stage 2 for office hours' filter (`REF:358`). Each new field carries a short note saying so, as the two overtime fields already carry theirs (TASK-14829). An administrator must not believe a change to the plus cap moved anyone's pay this month.
2. **AC's "default by analogy".** For staff on the AC agreement no central text says how a paid sick day counts. StatsTid applies the HK and PROSA rule (§ 7 nr. 2) as a default "by analogy", labelled so (ADR-043 D2, owner ruling D-8b = (a)). On every AC-family profile (`AC`, `AC_RESEARCH`, `AC_TEACHING`), the editor shows that label once, directly under the credit basis's heading (ruling 21).
3. **A warning, never a refusal, about office hours.** If the office hours entered do not add up to the weekly norm (37 hours, or the profile's own weekly norm where one is set), the editor says so, and still saves. Office hours that differ from the norm can be legitimate (ADR-043 D3; provisional item 14 asks an expert how they scale for part-timers).
4. **"Kontortid" is offered only when it can be saved.** While no weekday has both times filled, the "Kontortid" credit basis is disabled and a hint says why (ruling 21): wave 6 ends with "a screen never offers an action the server refuses".

**Worked example.** STY01's LocalAdmin opens the AC/OK24 profile. Below the existing fields a new card, "Institutionens egne regler for flex, kontortid og betalt fravær", lists the seven flex settings, office hours per weekday and the credit basis, each with the same small note. At first the "Kontortid" credit basis is disabled, with the hint "Kræver kontortid for mindst én ugedag.". She enters Monday to Thursday 08:00–15:30 and Friday 08:00–14:00, and the option becomes available. That is 36 hours; the profile sets no weekly norm of its own, so the editor compares with 37 and shows the warning "Kontortiden er i alt 36 timer om ugen, men den ugentlige norm er 37 timer. Du kan stadig gemme." She picks "Kontortid" as the credit basis; because this is an AC profile, the analogy label sits under the credit basis's heading. She saves: the request goes through (the server never refuses a sum). The history row of the saved profile then lists the new settings, for example "mandag 08:00–15:30, …" for the office hours. A client that sends "Kontortid" with no office hours anyway is still refused by the server (400, `OFFICE_HOURS_WITHOUT_WINDOWS`), and the editor keeps that refusal mapped to its Danish message under the credit basis.

**What does not change.** The four existing fields, their "Overskriv central" toggles, the two overtime notes (NR-P13), the Monday-only date picker (it still follows the weekly norm only), the 412 banner, and the history row's four existing lines and their ASCII labels (it gains nine lines below them).
