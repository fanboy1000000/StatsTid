## Plain language — why

The 11-hour rest rule lives in two places: the server's check (the authority, which the manager sees) and Skema's own warning in the day panel, shown instantly while the employee types, before anything is saved. TASK-14843 rewrote the server's check to the authority's test: **at the end of each piece of work, look 24 hours back; the longest unbroken rest in that stretch must be at least 11 hours.** This task gives the screen the same rule, so the two keep agreeing on the shared table.

**What the employee sees differently.**
- **Long days and split days now warn.** One shift of 16 hours, or 08–12 plus 17–22, or a day shift and a night shift on the same day, used to give no warning on the screen (and none on the server). Now they warn.
- **The warning names the moment that is judged.** "Denne registrering giver kun 8 timers sammenhængende hvile i de 24 timer før arbejdet slutter onsdag 11. marts kl. 14:00." (the final copy, item 17).
- **Danish time, really.** The night the clocks go forward is one hour shorter, the night they go back one hour longer, whatever time zone the laptop is set to.
- **The number is rounded down, once,** as on the server: a rest of 8 h 29 min 50 s shows 8,4, and a breach never shows the minimum itself (10 h 59 min shows 10,9).
- **A browser without Danish time-zone data says so** instead of looking compliant: one informational line, "Hviletiden kan ikke kontrolleres i denne browser, fordi tidszonedata for Danmark mangler."

**Worked example.** Mette types Tuesday 10 March 22:00–23:59 and Wednesday 11 March 06:00–14:00. *Today* the panel says "kun 6 timers hvile mellem tirsdag 10. marts og onsdag 11. marts". *After this task* both days' panels say "kun 8 timers sammenhængende hvile i de 24 timer før arbejdet slutter onsdag 11. marts kl. 14:00": in the 24 hours before Wednesday 14:00, the longest unbroken rest is Tuesday 14:00–22:00. The server reports the same 8,0 dated Wednesday.

**What does not change.** No server call is added (the instant warning on unsaved edits is the point). The fixed 11 hours (the served minimum is TASK-14829's). No derogation and no voluntary logic on the screen (TASK-14814's Step 5a ruling 6). The live region and the "not checked" notice (wave 3).
