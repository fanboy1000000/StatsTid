## Plain language — why
- **The defect.** Payroll decides which employment record applies to a stretch of days (a "piece") by looking only at its first day.
- **The case.** HR closes someone's profile inside a month, but the person stays employed (the close sets the profile's end date, not the employment end date). Payroll then pays the remaining days on the ended profile and tells nobody. That is a guessed record, which payroll's own rule forbids (`RecordGap`: a figure from a guessed record is worse than none).
- **The recheck inherits it.** TASK-14831's recheck re-runs the same derivation, so a close that wins the race against an export is recorded silently. `PayInputsLockRaceTests.ProfileCloseFirst_Export409` is red because of it.
- **Age.** The defect predates S148 (the start-date-only read dates from S137, `6f960ada`). It is registered as QUAL-265 at the merge.
- **The fix.** Payroll also checks the **last** day of every piece. If no complete record covers it, the month is refused by name through the existing `RecordGap`. No new cut point is added, so an unbroken timeline gives byte-identical pieces and no payslip moves.
