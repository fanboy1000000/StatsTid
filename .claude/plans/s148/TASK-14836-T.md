## Plain language — why

TASK-14836 lets HR record, for each person, the overtime regime (overtime, exempt, a merarbejde claim by contract or by discretion, or none) and whether they are on the flex scheme, dated, and read them as they stood on any day. You prove four things, each with a test that fails on today's code, where the routes do not exist:
- **AC-C11a:** HR records the facts from a date, supersedes them from a later date, and a read as of each day returns what held that day; each write leaves one audit row, one event and one audit-projection row.
- **AC-C11b:** HR editing their own facts gets 403 (GlobalAdmin too), a departed person is refused by the scope check, and HR of another organisation gets 403; each with nothing written.
- **AC-C11c:** a value the person's agreement does not allow (an AC value for an HK employee) is refused with 400, and a stored value that a later agreement no longer allows reads "not recorded" on the later days.
- **AC-C11d:** an edit dated inside a month does not split that month in payroll; the demo payslips do not move (PIN-1, at CI-W6).
