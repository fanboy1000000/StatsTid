## Plain language — why

TASK-14840 lets an employee (or HR for them) empty a Skema cell: an absence, a project entry, or a day's work periods. The removal is its own recorded event with an audit row; the registration it removes is kept and marked retired, never deleted; a removed holiday day is given back; and a removal survives a Backend restart.

This pass proves each of those promises with a test that fails on today's code, where the new `cleared` member is simply ignored. Four groups of facts carry most of the weight:
- **the basics:** each cell kind is removed, the event and the audit row are written, a removal of nothing records nothing, the locks and the authority rules hold;
- **the balance:** a removed holiday day gives back exactly what was stored, never refused for quota, given back once even when two removals race;
- **the paid-absence bound and the children's sick days:** the day locked by a typing mistake can now be repaired; day 1 of a child's illness cannot be removed while day 2 stands;
- **the restart:** the rebuild that runs at every Backend start must never bring a removed cell back, even when the removal was saved a moment before the restart.

Four mutations (M-18 … M-21) then show, in a CI evidence run, that the most delicate facts fail for the defect they exist for. Expected values come from the addendum (pasted in TASK-14840's brief), never from what the code returns.
