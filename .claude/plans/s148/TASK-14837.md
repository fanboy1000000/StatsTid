## Plain language — why

**What HR gets.** When an HR user opens a person in the "Organisation og medarbejdere" drawer, a new section, below the entitlement fields, shows the person's **overtime regime** and whether they are **on the flex scheme**, as recorded today, and lets HR record a new value from a date. TASK-14836 built the routes; you build the section.

**What it must also get right.**
1. **It offers only what the server accepts.** The regime list is the set the server serves for the person's agreement (HK: overtime or exempt; PROSA: overtime or contractual; AC: contractual, discretionary or none), never a hard-coded list; the date cannot be later than today, nor earlier than the current record's start (as TASK-14836's BC-3 is ruled). A refusal that still comes back is shown in Danish on its field.
2. **"Missing" is visible.** A person with no regime recorded, or whose recorded value their current agreement no longer allows, shows the regime as missing (HRP-025's surfacing; `PLAN:382`). Every AC person `init.sql` seeds starts that way (TASK-14836's item 9).
3. **One's own record is locked.** The server refuses anyone editing their own facts (TASK-14836, AC-C11b). On one's own record the section's inputs and its save button are disabled and point to the drawer's existing note, `pd-self-lock-note` (TASK-14846; the note already says why: the fields "har betydning for din egen løn"). The link is **merged** with any other description an input has, never overwritten (the S148 wave-5 batch-D brief adjudication, ruling 21).
4. **It does not disturb the drawer.** The section loads and saves on its own (BC-1); its loading or failure never blocks or fails the drawer's "Gem ændringer".

**Worked example.** Lise (LocalHR, STY01) opens Jonas (HK). The section reads `GET …/hr-facts?asOf=2026-11-10`: regime `OVERTIME`, on flex, recorded from Monday 2 March 2026, `liveVersion` 1, allowed `["OVERTIME","EXEMPT"]`. She chooses "exempt", "not on flex", from Monday 6 April 2026, and saves: the section sends `PUT …/hr-facts` with `If-Match: "1"` and `{ effectiveFrom: "2026-04-06", overtimeRegime: "EXEMPT", onFlexScheme: false }`, re-reads, and announces it. Opening her own record, Lise sees the section with its inputs disabled.
