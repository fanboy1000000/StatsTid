## Plain language — why

TASK-14834 lets an institution's local administrator record the institution's customary days (5 June from noon, 24 December, a closure day after Ascension), stop one from a date and bring it back from a later date. You prove two things with tests that fail on today's code, where the routes do not exist and every request answers 404:
- **AC-C10a:** each of the three writes lands its row (or row change), one audit row, one `CustomaryDayChanged` event and one audit-projection row that the institution's administrators can see, and a reinstatement leaves the withdrawn period visible as a gap.
- **AC-C10b:** an administrator of another institution, or one whose only covering scope is not an administrator's, is refused with 403 and nothing is written; a row of one institution cannot be reached through another institution's address.

**Worked example (AC-C10a).** The clock is Tuesday 10 November 2026. A LocalAdmin of STY01 creates "5 June, paid day off from 12:00, given to all, valid from Monday 5 January 2026": 201, `version` 1. She withdraws it from Monday 2 November 2026: 200, `version` 2, `validTo` 2026-11-02. She reinstates it from Tuesday 10 November 2026: 201, a **new** custom id at `version` 1, valid from 2026-11-10, while the old row still ends on 2 November. Three audit rows (`CREATED`, `WITHDRAWN`, `REINSTATED`), three events and three projection rows targeting STY01.
