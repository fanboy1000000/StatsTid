## Plain language — why

**What HR gets.** From stage 2 the pay rules need two facts StatsTid cannot work out by itself, because it holds no salary: a person's **overtime regime** (an HK employee earns +50 % overtime unless their pay puts them above the ceiling, "exempt"; a PROSA employee in pay groups 3–4 has a merarbejde claim instead; an AC employee's right depends on their category) and whether the person is **on the flex scheme** at all (local agreements leave some positions out). ADR-043 D4 and D2 make HR record both, dated and audited, per person (HRP-025, HRP-026). Wave 4 built the table (`init.sql:1031-1045`); this task builds the **routes** (read as of a day, and write), the **reader** that answers "what was true on day X", and the **starting values** for the people already in the system.

**What it must also get right.**
1. **Nobody records their own facts.** The regime will decide the person's own pay, so an HR user or administrator editing their own facts gets 403, whatever their role, and a second administrator must do it (RES-003; the S147 four-eyes ruling; the refinement's "self-decision helper", `ApprovalSelfGuard.IsSelf`).
2. **Only HR of the person's organisation, and never a departed person's.** The routes are `HROrAbove` and checked through the scope validator's employee check at the LocalHR floor, which also refuses a departed person acting on their own record and treats someone past the leaver grace as gone (`OrgScopeValidator.cs:142-227`).
3. **A value must fit the person's agreement.** HK allows "overtime" or "exempt"; PROSA "overtime" or "contractual" (groups 3–4); AC "contractual", "discretionary" or "none" (`REF:513`, provisional). A value outside that set is refused (400). And if a person later changes agreement, a stored value the new agreement does not allow reads as "not recorded" for the days under the new agreement (AC-C11c).
4. **Every write is a row, an audit row and an event, in one transaction**, with the audit-projection row (ADR-018 D3, ADR-026).
5. **No pay effect and no pay machinery.** Nothing reads either fact in S148; each joins pay with its first reader, flex membership in stage 1 and the regime in stage 2 (R-21, `REF:520`). So these writes take **no** pay-inputs lock, land **no** worklist row and are **not** frozen into a month's proof; an edit is **not** a payroll boundary (it is a separate table precisely so that it is not, `REF:512`) (AC-C11d).

**Worked example** (the clock Tuesday 10 November 2026; Lise, LocalHR of STY01; Jonas, an HK employee of STY01 with no facts recorded).
- *Record.* Lise records Jonas as "overtime", on the flex scheme, from Monday 2 March 2026 (`If-None-Match: *`). 200: one row (`overtime_regime` `OVERTIME`, `on_flex_scheme` true, from 2026-03-02, version 1); one audit row `CREATED`; one `EmployeeHrFactsChanged` event `CREATED` on `employee-hr-facts-<Jonas>`; one projection row targeting STY01.
- *Supersede.* Jonas's pay crosses the HK ceiling from Monday 6 April 2026; Lise records "exempt", not on flex, from that date (`If-Match: "1"`). 200: the first row now ends on 2026-04-06; a new row from 2026-04-06 at version 2; audit `SUPERSEDED`; event `SUPERSEDED` with `PrecedingFactsId` the first row.
- *Read as of a day.* As of Friday 3 April 2026: overtime, on flex. As of Monday 6 April 2026: exempt, not on flex.
- *Refused.* Lise recording "discretionary" for Jonas: 400, an AC value outside HK's set. Lise recording her own facts: 403, nothing written.
