## Plain language — why
- **The seeder.** TASK-14849 stops the start-up seeder from giving a full-time default profile to a still-employed person whose profile HR closed (QUAL-267). Your facts pin three things:
  - that case is left alone;
  - a person with no profile at all is still backfilled;
  - payroll's refusal of the uncovered month (QUAL-265) survives a seeder run.
- **The parity fixture.** It compares the screens' month reader with payroll's day reader. Every overlap in it today has one shape: the later-starting row also ends earlier and is inserted later. So a reader ordered by end date, or by insertion order, would still pass. One partial overlap, where the later-starting row ends later, closes that gap.
