## Plain language — why

**The problem.** Each employee has one balance record per holiday type and year. It holds how many days are used (`used`) and the year's entitlement (`total_quota`, 25 for a full year of *Ferie*). Four places in the code create that record when it is missing. All four get the record wrong in at least one way:

1. **`used` starts at 0, whatever is already booked.** In a rare state the record is missing although holidays are booked: the holiday was booked while the type had no configuration, and the configuration was added later. The next change then creates the record at "0 used" plus that change, so every earlier booking is forgotten. This is QUAL-196, registered since S145.
2. **The profile revaluation sets the total to 0.** When HR backdates a change of working hours, the recalculation can create a record with `total_quota = 0`. The team overview then shows "of 0 days", and the remaining figure stays negative for good, because nothing later corrects a total.
3. **The year-end carryover copies the closing year's total.** When the year-end settlement carries days over, it creates next year's record with the total of the year being closed. If the entitlement changed when the new year began (say 25 → 26), next year keeps 25.

**A worked example.** Mette is an HK employee, full time. In the holiday year that began 1 September 2025 she has *Ferie* booked on Wednesday 10 and Thursday 11 June 2026, but her (Ferie, 2025) record is missing. She books Friday 12 June. Today the record is created with `used` 1,0, although three days are booked, so the screen offers her two days too many. After this task it is created with `used` 3,0: the sum of her actual current bookings for that type and year.

**Why now, and why before clearing.** TASK-14840 lets an employee remove a booked day and gives the day back. On a missing record that removal would end at −1 day used. The owner chose to fix the cause in S148 (Q1, 2026-11-11, "Fix it in S148 (Recommended)"), and to fix the total in the same task (Q4, 2026-11-11, "Include it"). From this task on, every place that creates a record reads the year's entitlement the same way: the configuration in force **on the first day of that record's own holiday year** (cycle-3 rulings R1, R2). So whichever place happens to create the record, it gets the same total.

**What does not change.** A record that already exists is never re-seeded; it keeps today's "add the change" behaviour. No floor (clamp) is added: with the seed fixed there is no inconsistency to hide. Old records are not repaired (reseed, don't migrate). No screen and no API contract changes.
