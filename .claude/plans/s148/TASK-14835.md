## Plain language — why

**What a local administrator gets.** On the configuration page ("Konfiguration", `/lokal/ok-konfiguration`, LocalAdmin and above, `frontend/src/App.tsx:143-147`), under the local profile, a new section lists the institution's customary days: which day (5 June every year, or a single date such as Friday 15 May 2026), whether it is a paid day off or a closure day, how much of the day, for a paid day whether everyone gets it or it is on request, which agreement it applies to, and from when (and until when) it is valid. She can record a new one, withdraw one from a date, and reinstate a withdrawn one from a later date. TASK-14834 built the routes; you build the screen.

**What it must also get right.**
1. **It never offers what the server refuses** (wave 6's rule; `PLAN:405`, "no action the server refuses (from 14838)", and the wave-6 batch-A brief adjudication, ruling 21). The form shows the clock-time field only for "from a clock time", the hours field only for "a number of hours", the paid-mode choice only for a paid day off; a February 30 cannot be picked; a withdrawal date cannot be on or before the custom's start; "reinstate" is offered only on a withdrawn custom, and not before its end date. A refusal that still comes back (a race, an old tab) is shown in Danish on its field, never as raw English.
2. **It says customs do not yet affect pay.** Nothing reads customs for pay until stage 1 (`REF:35`, `:508`). One note says so (BC-2).
3. **Accessible** (`PLAN:405`): every input has a tied label; a radio group is a `<fieldset>` with a `<legend>` and its description on the fieldset (the batch-A ruling 17: no kit change); a success or failure is announced in a live region.

**Worked example.** Mette, LocalAdmin of STY01, picks STY01 on the page. The section lists nothing. She records "Hvert år: 5. juni · Betalt fridag · Fra kl. 12:00 · Gives til alle · Alle overenskomster · Gælder fra 5. januar 2026". The editor sends `{ recurringMonth: 6, recurringDay: 5, kind: "PAID_DAY_OFF", part: "FROM_TIME", partFromTime: "12:00:00", paidMode: "GIVEN_TO_ALL", validFrom: "2026-01-05" }`, the server answers 201, the list shows the custom and a status line confirms it. Later she withdraws it from Monday 2 November 2026; the row then shows "Gælder til 2. november 2026" and a "Genindfør" action whose date cannot be before 2 November.
