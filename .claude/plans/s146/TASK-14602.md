## Plain language — why

Two screens tell a part-time employee, and their manager, that the month's expected hours are a full-timer's: the balance summary and the manager's team overview compute "hours expected this month" as weekdays ÷ 5 × the agreement's weekly norm, ignoring the part-time fraction. For the stored 0.811 fraction, a 22-weekday month shows 162.80 where the engine judges 22 × 6.00 = 132.00. The owner ruled (S145 Q-1, 2026-10-14) that S146 fixes it as its own task, with the person's own norm, fraction at month end. It uses the engine's own per-day helper, so screen and engine agree. (Public holidays still do not reduce the norm; that is a registered model finding for the S147 program, which will change both together.)
