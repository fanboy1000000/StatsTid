## Plain language — why

Three places show a flex balance that has been 0 for everyone. From now on the server sends the employee's latest **paid** month's closing balance and which month that is, or nothing when no month has been paid. The screens say so honestly: the balance "pr. <month>", or "ingen afregnet måned" (no settled month). On the leader's team overview, a row whose month the employee has not submitted stays hidden as today (OQ-5), and is now visibly different from "no settled month". The year tile's sub-label "optjent overtid" (overtime earned) sits on a flex figure: that is the very flex-versus-overtime confusion this sprint registers, so it is corrected. A dead card and hook, which no page imports, are deleted.
