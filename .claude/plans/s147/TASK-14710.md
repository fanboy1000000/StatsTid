## Plain language — why

Today the system switches a leaver off the day after their last working day. From then on nobody can approve their final month, and payroll will not pay it. The owner ruled (OQ-2) that the leaver's **manager** keeps 5 calendar days to act as today, and that HR takes over from day 6 (TASK-14713). So the automatic switch-off moves from day 1 to day 6. That would also keep the **leaver** able to log in and change their own data for 5 more days, which is not intended: the leaver's own access must still end on day 1. This task does both halves. It moves the switch-off behind one named constant, refuses the leaver's login from day 1, and refuses every write an ordinary employee can make to their own record from day 1, whatever the leaver's role. It also retires two deprecated project routes that key on the caller and could not carry the rule. (The same rule for admin routes is already in place from TASK-14703.)
