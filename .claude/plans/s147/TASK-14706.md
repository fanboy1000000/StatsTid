## Plain language — why

The 11-hour daily rest check can only measure a gap between two clock times. Days registered in Skema carry hours but no clock times, so the check silently skips them, and the team overview shows "no breach" for days nobody checked (QUAL-234, Critical). The real fix (clock times reaching payroll and the check) is an owner decision in the decide track (D-2) and an S148 build. Until then, this small fix makes the check honest: it reports which days it could not analyse, and both screens show one month-level notice, "Hviletid ikke kontrolleret for N dage". No pay figure changes.
