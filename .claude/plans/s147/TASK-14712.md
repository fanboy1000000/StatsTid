## Plain language — why

The 5-day grace keeps a leaver switched on until day 6 so that **their manager** can still sign their final month. If the leaver is themselves a manager, that would also keep them in charge of **their own reports** for 5 days after they have left. The grace exists for the leaver's manager, not for the leaver's authority, so a departed approver must stop counting as an approver on day 1, exactly as today, and their reports' months must move to the next manager at once. This task changes who counts as an "acting user" at the five places that decide approver authority: active **and** not past their end date. It only ever removes authority. It does **not** change stand-ins: the owner ruled (Q-B = (c)) that a stand-in a manager or HR appointed before the manager left keeps approving until its end date or until HR revokes it, and `/remove` closes it. A stand-in a departed manager tries to create **after** leaving is refused by TASK-14703.

## Contract (the WHAT)

1. **"Acting user" = `is_active` AND (`employment_end_date` IS NULL OR `employment_end_date` ≥ the operation's date)**, at the five reads. The date is the operation's own (the authorizer's `asOf`, the resolver's `effectiveAsOf`, the CTE's `@today`), never a second clock read (PAT-028). *Hint:* widen `IsUserActiveAsync` to take the date (or add `IsActingUserAsync(userId, asOf, ct)` and move the three resolver calls to it); the prefetch also reads `employment_end_date`.
2. **The effect** is to remove authority only, and to restore today's D+1 timing: from D+1 the departed manager is skipped and routing escalates (the step-4 walk), and the pending list and the resolver agree ("see = act").
3. **The audit event is unchanged (R-29 N-4):** `ReportingLineManagerDeactivated` still fires on D+6 from Step A; nothing emits a "deactivated" event for an active row. The D+1 routing is reconstructable from `EmployeeEmploymentEndDateSet` plus this rule (stated in the ADR-040 amendment, O-6).
4. **The delegator side (R-31's census item): no change (P-2; owner Q-B = (c)).** The resolver's vikar consult (`ReportingLineRepository.cs:1206-1213`), the unit-leader stand-in reads (`DesignatedApproverAuthorizer.cs:414-422`; `IAuthorityFactsSource.cs:204-210`) and the pending-list stand-in legs (`ApprovalPeriodRepository.cs:372-377`, `:409-423`) are **not** touched: a stand-in appointed before the manager left keeps approving until its end date or HR's revocation, and `/remove` closes it. A stand-in a departed manager tries to create after leaving is refused by TASK-14703 (P-1, B14 and B16). TASK-14712-T pins this behaviour as two boundary facts.
