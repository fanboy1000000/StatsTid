## Plain language — why

Two raw payroll routes accept pay lines that the caller wrote, with no approved month and no second person (SEC-054). The owner ruled to retire them (OQ-3 = (a)). But they are today the only way to pay one kind of month: a month that comes **before** an employee's first recorded flex month and has no record yet ("case 3a"), which the calculating route refuses. So, first, that month gets a proper path, as the owner chose (remedy (i)): it is paid through the calculating route, and its flex is marked "not tracked" rather than invented. A month with no pay lines writes nothing at all, as ADR-034 already says for that case. The routes themselves are deleted in the next task, only after this is proven in CI. This reverses S146's ruled refusal of case 3a, by the owner's choice; the cost is that such a month's flex is not carried forward.
