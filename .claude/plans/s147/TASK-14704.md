## Plain language — why

Both state tenders require four eyes on a month: the person who signs it as sent must not also sign it as approved. Today the only separation check compares the approver with the **employee**, so an HR user who sends a month on someone's behalf can then approve it themselves. The owner ruled strict four-eyes with no exception (OQ-1 = (a)): approve and reject are refused for the sender; reopening stays allowed because it signs nothing and clears both stamps. The rule is checked three times: at the endpoint, inside the transaction that writes the approval, and again when payroll pays the month. The demo loader, which signs both parts with one login, gets a second signer.

## Contract (the WHAT)

1. **The sender** of a month = `EmployeeApprovedBy`, or `SubmittedBy` on a legacy row where the former is null.
2. **The endpoint guard.** Approve and reject refuse the sender (Ordinal; a null sender never matches) with **403** and a date-free reason ("the sender of a month cannot approve or reject it (four-eyes)"), placed **where SEC-009's guard sits**: after the row load, **before the status check**, so a sender on an APPROVED month gets 403, not 409. A structured WARNING log (`SEC-053 sender-decision denied …`); no audit row (OQ-2 of S130). *Hint:* a new `src/Backend/StatsTid.Backend.Api/Endpoints/Helpers/ApprovalSenderGuard.cs` beside `ApprovalSelfGuard` (do **not** edit `ApprovalSelfGuard.cs`: TASK-14705 owns it this wave).
3. **Inside the transaction.** The conditional UPDATE for APPROVED and REJECTED gains a predicate on the locked row: the sender is distinct from the actor (`COALESCE(employee_approved_by, submitted_by) IS DISTINCT FROM @actorId`). A row refused by that predicate is never updated, and the handler answers **403 with the four-eyes reason**, distinguishable from the source-state 409 (*Hint:* RETURN the snapshotted sender beside the old status, or re-read under the lock).
4. **At payroll.** `AssertPeriodApprovedForUpdateAsync` also reads `approved_by`, `employee_approved_by`, `submitted_by` and `employee_id`, and throws the existing conflict (409) when `approved_by` equals the sender or the employee. A null sender passes.
5. **Reopen is not guarded (R-6).**
6. **The demo loader** approves and rejects as a **second identity** that is not the sender. *Hint:* a second login on a separate API client for the approve/reject acts; if the manifest has no suitable second privileged identity, add one in the generator, and say so (the manifest is regenerated by the tool, never hand-edited).
