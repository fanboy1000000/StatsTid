## Plain language — why

With a leaver's final month payable by HR (TASK-14713) and the month before the ledger start payable untracked (TASK-14715), nothing needs the two raw routes any more. They take pay lines the caller wrote, with no approved month behind them and no second person, so they are a single-person path to payroll (SEC-054). This task deletes them and the dead Orchestrator task type that pointed at one of them, and makes the export service require an approved period, so every export record now comes from an approved month with two signers.
