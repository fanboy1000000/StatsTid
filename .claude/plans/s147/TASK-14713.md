## Plain language — why

From day 6 a leaver is switched off, and today that means nobody can approve, reject or reopen their final month, and payroll refuses to pay it (QUAL-230). The owner ruled: from day 6, **HR only**. This task lets an HR-tier actor (and only one) address a switched-off leaver on exactly the surfaces that finish the final month: approve, reject, reopen's leader arm, the Skema month view, and payroll's calculate-and-export. A leaver is admitted only for a month in which they were actually employed, and that check runs after the access check, so nobody learns an employment date by being refused. HR's leaver list gets the actions it needs and shows, on each row, the date the manager's window closed. The four-eyes rule from TASK-14704 still holds: if HR sent the month, a second HR person approves it.
