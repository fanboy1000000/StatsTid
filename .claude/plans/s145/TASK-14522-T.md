## Plain language — why

Until now a set of tests pinned "payroll refuses a month with a mid-month change". That was the registered limitation QUAL-149, and one of the tests even says "if this turns red because the month now calculates … retire this pin". TASK-14522 lifts the limitation, so these tests now pin the opposite:
- the calculating routes pay such a month, part by part;
- the raw export routes refuse it with their own reason;
- an agreement change in the middle of a week is refused by the week check rather than the planner.

This pass changes exactly the listed tests, labels every changed value with `// S145 B2:` (a Stage B label in R27's list, R-65; "B2" names refinement § B2, not wave B.2), and writes no production code.
