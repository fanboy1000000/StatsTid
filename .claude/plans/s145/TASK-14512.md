   - **Admission is split from evaluation (R42).** `CalculateWithOutcomeAsync` is split into two steps:
     - *admission:* the planner refusal, the week check (with R43's filter), the prior-month check and the range hours read (`ReadCurrentAsync`);

7. **Close the reopen-vs-export race (added at TASK-14509's Step 5a, 2026-10-14: adjudicator R-4, Codex WARNING, Fable 5a).**
   - **The gap.** M's reopen locks M's approval row and reads no M+1 export. An export of M+1 locks M+1's row. Today it does not check M at all; after Do 2 it checks M's approval in PCS admission, *before* its transaction, and does not lock it. (Wording corrected at 14509's Step 5a cycle 3.) Until Do 2 lands, the gap is wider than a race: a reopen that commits before the export leaves an export on an unapproved M with no row, which is the case Do 2's 409 closes. In a millisecond interleaving both commit, so October is exported on September's APPROVED state that a reopen is withdrawing, and no `PRIOR_MONTH_REOPENED` worklist row is written.
   - **The fix.** The calculating export route acquires M's (the prior month's) approval-row lock and rechecks M's approval *inside* its transaction, holding the lock through the export commit. The read of M's status must be a **row lock** (`FOR UPDATE` or `FOR SHARE`), not a plain SELECT under READ COMMITTED; a plain read would not serialize against the reopen (Fable 5a of 14509, WARNING 1). Use the same lock order as the reopen, prior month before current, so the two cannot deadlock. This is the same place as this task's prior-month 409 (Do 2), so the 409 is decided under the lock.
   - **Pin**, if a deterministic one is feasible: two transactions stepped by hand. Otherwise reviewed, not pinned (as 14507's pre-check race was). Say which in the report.
