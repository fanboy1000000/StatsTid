## Plain language — why this task exists

After wave B.1, overtime, flex and the payroll norm rule carry the new "timeline-aware" label. Payroll still refuses split months, though, because it hands the planner the Rule Engine's **whole** rule list. That list includes three rules payroll never runs (the weekly norm and two rest rules), and those keep their "never cut in two" label. This task makes payroll hand the planner **only the rules it runs**. That one change is what makes a month with a mid-month change in hours, position or agreement payable.

Most of the rest is already in place from Stage A, and this brief says so explicitly so it is not rebuilt:
- **The whole month is already evaluated in one call across all its parts.** The per-plan lane sends one piece per EMPLOYED segment (`PeriodCalculationService.cs:752-792, :945-947`).
- **Each line is already mapped under the codes of the part of the month it falls in.** The timeline's lines are split by date into each segment (`SplitBySegment`, `:3561-3576`), and each segment maps its own lines under its own dated wage-type key (`:1009-1010`, key from A4, `:1675-1677`).
- **An agreement change in the middle of a week already refuses** wherever it falls, inside the month included (`EnsureNoMidWeekAgreementChange`, `:3380-3414`, over every settled week). Today the planner's refusal simply answers first for an interior change. Once the planner stops refusing, the week check answers: the "interior residual" (refinement `:698`).

Three things are new:
1. **The merge of the three timeline rules** must take the timeline's single answer, not the per-segment pieces it was cut into for mapping. Otherwise TASK-14520's new merger rightly fails them (R-2, the B.0 planning input; ruled R-75).
2. **The raw export routes** (`/export`, `/export-period`) map every line under the request's one profile. They cannot export a month with two parts correctly, so they refuse it under a separate, named reason (Q6 (a); ruled R-74, R-76).
3. **A timeline line dated in no employed part of the month now fails loudly** (ruled R-56). Today such a line is silently filed under a neighbouring part, which would be a wrong wage-type code reported as success. It cannot happen today (one employment spell per user, and the engine dates lines only on covered days inside the period), so the refusal costs nothing, and it turns a future breach of the engine's contract into a 500 with nothing written.

## Contract (the WHAT)

1. **The evaluated-set filter (B1, `:432`).** Planning receives only the classifications whose `RuleId` is one payroll evaluates: `NORM_CHECK_37H`, `OVERTIME_CALC`, `FLEX_BALANCE`, `SUPPLEMENT_CALC`, `ON_CALL_DUTY`, `ABSENCE_CALC`. It is applied in `BuildPlanWithHistoriesAsync` (so both the calculating path and the raw-route guard plan with it), as one named member, so the list and its reason live in one place. A classification-fetch failure still throws `RuleClassificationsUnavailableException` (the 503) before any filtering. The merge (`:880`) and `FromManifest` (`:2104`) may keep the unfiltered list; they see only rules that produced results.
2. **One merge input for a single-plan rule (the R-2 input; ruled at B.0, R-75: stands as drafted).** For each rule whose resolved strategy is `SinglePlanEvaluation`, `MergePerSegmentRuleResults` is given **the one row the timeline call returned for it** (unsplit: all its lines, its period figures), stamped with the manifest id. It is **not** given the per-segment rows that the mapping used. The merger then passes that one row through, so the criterion "the merger throws on two" (`:684`) holds literally, and there is still **one mapping path** (the per-segment split stays, R-2's reason). A rule with any other strategy merges its per-segment rows exactly as today; the one-segment fast path (`:2396-2397`) is unchanged. A `SinglePlanEvaluation` rule with no timeline row (a fixture marking another rule timeline-aware) merges its per-segment rows and fails loudly on two. That is never silent.
3. **Q6 (a), the raw routes (`:442`, `:518`; ruled at B.0, R-76).** After the week check, `EnsurePeriodCalculableAsync` refuses a plan with **≥ 2 EMPLOYED segments** by a new reason: `PeriodNotCalculableException` with reason **`split-month-single-profile-route`**.

Use the calculating routes (recalculate, or calculate-and-export) to pay the period."*
